## [2026.9.0a1] - 2026-09-25

### Added

- Passive RS485 listen sketch: port, byte buffer, and `RegisterUpdate` bus.
- Unimplemented codec (`ProtocolUnknownError`, `CODEC_IMPLEMENTED = False`).
- CI, docs, issue templates, and repository hardening script.

This pre-release reserves the PyPI name. It does not decode the bus.
